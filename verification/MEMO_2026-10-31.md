## 2. J6 action tables (cleared: distribution-level only; repair verified on all 400 records)
Source: out/actions_repaired (J6 18497965, step lists truncated to n_steps). Script part4/j6_tables.py, JSON part4/j6_tables.json.
These are action statistics from a fresh evaluation replay on the same 400 test images, not read from the saved counterfactuals.
Definitions: sd is the population sd over all env steps. "between-share (multi-step)" = b/(b+w) over images with >1 step
(b = var of per-image means, w = mean within-image var). This is the definition behind the 0.67-0.78 range; n/a for n→p,
which has only 1 multi-step image. "eta²" = between-image SS / total SS over all steps of all images. For n→p it is 1.00
trivially, because 199/200 images contribute a single step. "sat lo/hi" = share of steps with sigmoid(a) ≤ 0.01 / ≥ 0.99,
i.e. the control within 1% of its range from the lower/upper bound. For a4/a5 the control is the extra-pass indicator,
so its "mean" is P(extra pass on).
