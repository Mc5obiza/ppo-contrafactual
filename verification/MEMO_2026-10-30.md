### 2.6 Does the learned policy add measurable value?

**Over the no-policy arm: yes, in p→n, by a large margin. In n→p: no.**
- p→n: CV +0.445 (0.225 → 0.670; 90 images gained validity, 1 lost) and LHR lift +0.532
  [+0.423, +0.647], d=0.76. That is about 30× the rerun noise floor (+0.016). Transfer p→n mean 0.169 → 0.276
  (point estimates only).
- n→p: CV 0.940 vs 0.945 (4 vs 3 discordant). Transfer n→p mean 0.307 vs 0.281, nih 0.101 vs 0.044
  (point estimates, no CIs), and FID is higher under the policy (22.4 vs 15.9). Nothing measurable favours the
  policy in this direction.

**Over the fixed schedule at the policy's mean action: not established.**
- The constant action recovers 86% of the policy's LHR gain over no-policy (0.456 of 0.532) and 87%
  of its CV p→n gain (0.385 of 0.445).
- What remains: LHR +0.076 [−0.008, +0.173], p=0.11, d=0.13. CV p→n +0.060 (22 vs 10 discordant, p=0.050,
  uncorrected; the rerun pair J4/J6 shows 15 discordant images). n→p: identical validity on every image.
  The transfer p→n mean is 0.276 vs 0.246, with no CI available.
- Two caveats cut in unknown directions: J5b's p→n constant is a near-mean, not the mean (bug), and the
  arms ran on mixed GPUs.

In plain terms: the measured value of the learned policy is that training found a much better operating point
than the generator's default schedule, for p→n. Per-image, per-step adaptation adds at most a small amount,
not distinguishable from zero with these data. Not tested: whether a cheap tuned fixed schedule (grid or random
search, no RL) would find the same point.

### 2.7 Two abstracts (both use J4 numbers; both ≤200 words)

**A — method-first (195 words)**

> Diffusion-based counterfactual explainers for medical image classifiers usually apply one hand-tuned sampling schedule to every image. We present PPO-CF, in which a PPO agent sets the sampling controls of a frozen, LoRA-adapted latent diffusion generator (noise strength, guidance scales, refinement passes, ramp-up and stopping), with a soft background lock that keeps edits largely inside a dilated lung region. On the RSNA Pneumonia Detection Challenge, on identical test images and seeds, the learned controller raises opacity-removal (p→n) validity from 0.225 under the generator's default schedule to 0.670, and lesion-targeting lift against radiologist boxes from 2.06× to 2.60× (paired d = 0.76, n = 148); n→p validity is unchanged (0.94). A fixed schedule set to the policy's mean action recovers most of this gain (0.610; 2.52×); the remaining benefit of per-image control is not distinguishable from rerun noise. Because validity shares a classifier with guidance, we pair it with a lesion hit rate and a held-out transfer ratio: edits stay diffuse, and n→p transfer to the most independent validator is adversarial (0.044). In a five-arm study (one run per arm), a sparsity reward (d = 0.36) and a Grad-CAM constraint (d = 1.07) both reduce lesion targeting.

**B — evaluation-first (200 words)**

> Counterfactual explanations for medical image classifiers are usually scored by Counterfactual Validity (CV), computed with the guiding classifier. We show that CV alone cannot establish that an edit is class-relevant, and pair it with two measurements free of that circularity: a Lesion Hit Rate (LHR) anchored to radiologist-drawn boxes, and a held-out transfer ratio that re-scores counterfactuals with classifiers never used for guidance. Our testbed is PPO-CF, a PPO agent that schedules the sampling of a frozen, LoRA-adapted latent diffusion generator. On the RSNA Pneumonia Detection Challenge it reaches a mean CV of 0.81 and a 2.60× LHR lift (n = 148), yet changes about 65% of pixels, and its n→p transfer to the most independent validator is adversarial (0.044). The policy beats the default schedule (LHR +0.53, paired d = 0.76), but a fixed schedule at its mean action comes within 0.08 (95% CI −0.01 to +0.17): the gain lies in the operating point, and per-image control adds nothing measurable. Across five trained arms (one run each), a sparsity reward (d = 0.36) and a Grad-CAM constraint (d = 1.07) both degrade lesion targeting. CV without an anchored companion metric overstates progress, as it does for our own system.
