# MEMO 2026-11-05: J15 Latent Shift smoke test (18537169), why CV = 0

Status: diagnostic only, 8 images per group. NOT cleared, not for the paper.

## Question
Smoke eval_metrics.json reports CV = 0.0 in both directions. Was this an eval/search mismatch?

## Answer: no mismatch, CV = 0 is real
- The search's own counter agrees: manifest `search.*.valid_by_search` = 0/8 in both directions.
  "2/8 p->n took lambda = 2.0" (JOBS_SUBMITTED note) meant two images fell back to the largest-shift
  lambda at the grid maximum. It did not mean two valid images.
- Search criterion (latent_shift_eval.py) = src/metrics.py counterfactual_validity: s*Delta >= tau = 0.8
  AND crossing 0.5, scored by the rsna+chex guidance ensemble.

## What fails is the margin, not the crossing (from latent_shift_aux.pt trajectories)
- n->p: f0 0.02-0.18. Best guidance shift 0.46-0.65 (mean 0.56), reached at lambda 0.05-0.3, then flat
  or falling. f at best 0.53-0.71: **8/8 cross 0.5**, 0/8 reach +0.8.
- p->n: f0 0.80-0.88. Best shift 0.31-0.50 (mean 0.42). f at best 0.35-0.53: **7/8 cross 0.5**, 0/8 reach -0.8.
  The AE round trip alone lowers f (f_rec mean 0.74 vs f0 0.84).
- Shifts saturate early along the one-gradient direction, so the grid extension to 3.0/4.0 in the
  full run (18537216) will not change CV. It is harmless.

## Other smoke numbers (n = 8, indicative only)
- Transfer n->p: nih 0.16, pc 0.37, mimic_ch -0.01, mean 0.17 (denominator 0.56, CI [0.52, 0.60]).
- LHR lift p->n: 1.47 vs J4 2.15 on the same 8 images (paired +0.68, CI [0.23, 1.25]; J4 better on 7/8).

## Implication for the paper
Under the paper's CV (tau = 0.8), single-gradient Latent Shift will very likely score about 0. A fair
table also needs the boundary-crossing rate (tau = 0) for both Latent Shift and J4 (CPU, from shards).
Open decision for the user: whether to add an iterative variant (gradient recomputed at each step) as a
stronger version of the baseline (GPU).
