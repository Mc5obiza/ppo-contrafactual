\subsection*{R1. Reward}
\label{sec:repro-reward}

Notation: $x$ is the episode's original image (fixed for the episode), $x_{t+1}$ the image after policy step $t$, $d_t = |f(x_t) - c|$ with $f$ the guidance classifier, $S$ the soft lung mask of $x$ (computed once per episode, Table~\ref{tab:repro-gen}), and $p_O(\cdot)$ the softmax, clamped to $[10^{-8}, 1]$, of the frozen YOLOv8n feature vector (Table~\ref{tab:repro-gen}). Means and sums run over the $256 \times 256$ pixels. The per-step reward is
\begin{equation*}
r_t = k_c \textstyle\sum_j \lambda_j R_j,
\end{equation*}
with $k_c = 2.25$ for n$\to$p ($c = 1$) and $k_c = 1$ for p$\to$n ($c = 0$), applied to every term.

$\lambda_{\text{sp}}(u) = 0$ outside the sparsity arm. \subsection*{R2. Action mapping}
\label{sec:repro-action}

The actor outputs the mean $\mu$ of a 9-dimensional diagonal Gaussian over a raw, unbounded vector $a \in \mathbb{R}^9$. During training $a$ is sampled; at evaluation $a = \mu$. Each control is a fixed transform of $a$, with $\sigma$ the logistic sigmoid (Table~\ref{tab:repro-action}). The raw vector is never clipped. Ranges are open intervals, because $\sigma$ never reaches 0 or 1.
% factory/ppo.py:271-276; factory/environment.py:479-498

\emph{Discrete controls.} No Bernoulli distribution is used. $a_4$ and $a_5$ are Gaussian like the other dimensions and are thresholded deterministically, $e_i = \mathbf{1}[\sigma(a) > 0.5] = \mathbf{1}[a > 0]$, identically in training and evaluation. The PPO log-probability is that of the raw Gaussian sample, with no correction for the sigmoid or the threshold. Stochasticity in $e_1, e_2$ comes only from Gaussian exploration during training.

\emph{Per-pass schedule.} Each policy step runs $n = 2 + e_1 + e_2$ chained generator passes, each taking the previous pass's output as its input. Pass $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ uses
\begin{align*}
s_k &= \min\big(0.9,\ s\,\beta\,(1 + 0.15k)\big), \\
g_{c,k} &= g_c\, b\,(1 + 0.35k), \\
g_{p,k} &= g_p\, \max(1/b,\ 0.6)\, \max(1 - 0.15k,\ 0.4),
\end{align*}
and the same ramp start $r$. The applied strength $s_k$ therefore ranges over $(0.17, 0.9]$ rather than Table~1's $(0.20, 0.70)$. Neither floor in $g_{p,k}$ binds: $1/b > 0.769$ and $1 - 0.15k \geq 0.55$ for $k \leq 3$.

\subsection*{R6. Metric definitions}
\label{sec:repro-metrics}

Notation: $X_n = \{x : f(x) < 0.2\}$ and $X_p = \{x : f(x) > 0.8\}$ are the first $N \leq 200$ images of each kind, in dataset order, selected by the guidance classifier $f$ rather than by label. $f$ is the ensemble's mean probability in ensemble arms. n$\to$p edits $X_n$ and p$\to$n edits $X_p$, with $s = +1$ and $s = -1$ respectively. Images are grayscale, $256 \times 256$, in $[0, 1]$, and $\delta = |x_c - x|$ per pixel.
% code: src/metrics.py:69-117 (scan_groups, shuffle=False), pipeline.py:112-119

\paragraph{Counterfactual Validity.}
\begin{equation*}
\mathrm{CV} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i} \mathbf{1}\big[s\,(f(x_c^i) - f(x^i)) \geq \tau \,\wedge\, B_i\big], \quad \tau = 0.8,
\end{equation*}
with $B_i$: $f(x^i) \leq 0.5 < f(x_c^i)$ for n$\to$p and $f(x^i) \geq 0.5 > f(x_c^i)$ for p$\to$n. Pairs with a non-finite score are dropped. Mean CV is the unweighted mean of the two directions. On $X_n$ and $X_p$ the margin condition already implies $B_i$: with $f \in [0, 1]$, $s\,(f(x_c^i) - f(x^i)) \geq 0.8$ forces $f(x_c) \geq 0.8$ for n$\to$p and $f(x_c) \leq 0.2$ for p$\to$n, and the source side holds by construction of the groups. The tightening relative to a direction-agnostic CV therefore comes from the sign requirement alone.
% code: src/metrics.py:181-236 (>= tau, signed), :1095-1097 (mean of directions)
% paper: sec/4_evaluation.tex Sec. 4.1, Eq. (cv) -- matches code (>=).
% report: PDF Ch. 4 Sec. I (p. 26) gives no formula ("fraction valid, tau = 0.8").
%         report/report.tex:363-366 (older draft) writes |f(x_c)-f(x)| > tau: strict and unsigned -- differs from code.

\paragraph{Fraction of pixels changed.} $\phi_i = \frac{1}{HW}\sum_p \mathbf{1}[\delta_p > 0.01]$, averaged over pairs. Pairs with $\sum_p \delta_p = 0$ are skipped, both here and for edit concentration.
% code: src/metrics.py:746-762
% report: PDF Ch. 4 Sec. III (p. 30) quotes the same quantity ("66% and 62% of all pixels changed").

\paragraph{Edit concentration.} $C_i = \sum_{p \in \mathrm{top}_k(\delta)} \delta_p \big/ \sum_p \delta_p$ with $k = \mathrm{round}(0.05 \cdot 65{,}536) = 3{,}277$, averaged over pairs. Spatially uniform change scores exactly 0.05. As an illustrative heuristic only, not a threshold, a compact lesion-shaped edit would be expected to put roughly half or more of its mass in the top 5\% of pixels. We do not use 0.5 as a decision boundary.
% code: src/metrics.py:716-763 (docstring: ">~0.5 is focal" -- uncalibrated heuristic)
% report: PDF Ch. 3 Sec. IV.4 (p. 24) and Ch. 4 Sec. III (p. 30): "A focal consolidation should concentrate well above 0.5" -- heuristic, no calibration preserved.

\paragraph{Identity preservation.} Both scores are computed per pair (source image vs.\ its counterfactual) and averaged. \emph{MS-SSIM}: five scales with $2 \times 2$ average pooling between them; an $11 \times 11$ Gaussian window, $\sigma = 1.5$, zero padding; $C_1 = 0.01^2$ and $C_2 = 0.03^2$ (data range 1); scale weights $(0.0448, 0.2856, 0.3001, 0.2363, 0.1333)$. The result is $\prod_{j=1}^{4} \mathrm{cs}_j^{w_j} \cdot \mathrm{ssim}_5^{w_5}$, with each factor clamped at $\geq 10^{-6}$. \emph{HF-corr}: $h(x) = x - G_{11, 1.5} * x$, $r$ the Pearson correlation of $h(x)$ and $h(x_c)$ over all pixels, reported as $(1 + r)/2$.
% code: src/metrics.py:592-709; five scales because floor(log2(256/11))+1 = 5 (metrics.py:632-634)
% report: PDF Ch. 4 Sec. I.1 (p. 26), qualitative description only, no parameters.

\paragraph{FID.} $\mathrm{FID}(A, B) = \lVert \mu_A - \mu_B \rVert^2 + \mathrm{Tr}\big(\Sigma_A + \Sigma_B - 2(\Sigma_A \Sigma_B)^{1/2}\big)$. Here $\Sigma$ is the unbiased sample covariance plus $10^{-6} I$, and the real part of the matrix square root is taken. Features are the global-average-pooled output (1024-d) of the \texttt{features} module of TorchXRayVision DenseNet-121 \texttt{densenet121-res224-rsna}, applied to the image rescaled to $[-1024, 1024]$ and resized bilinearly to $224^2$. The main paper reports the cross-group values $\mathrm{FID}(X_p, X_c^{\text{n}\to\text{p}})$ and $\mathrm{FID}(X_n, X_c^{\text{p}\to\text{n}})$, where the ``real target class'' is the classifier-selected group, not the label. The feature extractor is the \texttt{rsna} model, which also guides generation in the single-classifier and 2-model arms (membership of the 3-model ensemble: \TODO{not found}), so FID is not independent of guidance.

\paragraph{Held-out transfer ratio.} For validator $v \in \{\texttt{nih}, \texttt{pc}, \texttt{mimic\_ch}\}$ (TorchXRayVision DenseNet-121, Pneumonia output) and direction $s$,
\begin{equation*}
\rho_v = \frac{\frac{1}{N}\sum_i s\,\big(f_v(x_c^i) - f_v(x^i)\big)}{\frac{1}{N}\sum_i s\,\big(f_g(x_c^i) - f_g(x^i)\big)},
\end{equation*}
over the same $N$ images. $f_g$ is the guidance classifier (mean probability over members for an ensemble), and $\rho_v$ is undefined when the denominator's magnitude is at most $10^{-8}$. Any validator that is also a guidance member is dropped. The denominator must use the classifier(s) that actually guided generation.
