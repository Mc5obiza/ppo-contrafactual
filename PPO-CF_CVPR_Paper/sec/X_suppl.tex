\subsection*{S1. Reward}

The per-step reward is $r_t = k_c \sum_j \lambda_j R_j$, with $k_c = 2.25$ for n$\to$p ($c = 1$) and $k_c = 1$ for p$\to$n. Here $d_t = |f(x_t) - c|$, $x$ is the episode's original image, $S$ its lung mask (computed once per episode), and $p_O(\cdot)$ the softmax of the frozen YOLOv8n feature vector (\texttt{factory/environment.py}:353--467; weights \texttt{config.py}:291--302).

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.52\linewidth}l@{}}
\toprule
Term & Definition & Weight \\
\midrule
$R_{\text{prog}}$ & $d_t - d_{t+1}$ & 1.5 \\
$R_{\text{val}}$ & $-d_{t+1}$ & 2.0 \\
$R_{\text{close2}}$ & $-d_{t+1}^2$ & 2.5 \\
$R_{\text{fid}}$ & $-\operatorname{mean}|x_{t+1} - x|$ & 2.0 \\
$R_{\text{pres}}$ & $-\big(\sum S\,|x_{t+1} - x| / \sum S + \eta\,\mathrm{KL}(p_O(x)\,\|\,p_O(x_{t+1}))\big)$, $\eta = 0.5$ & 3.0 \\
$R_{\text{bud}}$ & $-1$ & 0.1 \\
$R_{\text{hit}}$ & $\mathbf{1}[d_{t+1} < 0.08]$ (the $L_1$ edit budget is $\infty$, so inactive) & 2.0 \\
$R_{\text{stop}}$ & $R_{\text{hit}} \cdot \mathbf{1}[\tau_t > 0.5]$ & 2.0 \\
$R_{\text{sparsity}}$ & $-\operatorname{mean}|x_{t+1} - x|$; $(1-\text{CAM})$-weighted only when a Grad-CAM mask is active & $\lambda_{\text{sp}}$ (sparsity arm) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Reward terms and weights.}
\end{table*}

$\lambda_{\text{sp}} = 0$ except in the sparsity arm, where it ramps linearly from 0 to 2.0 between 20\% and 60\% of the PPO updates. A safeguard measures CV every 10 updates on 24 held-out far-pool images (at most 4 steps each) and freezes $\lambda_{\text{sp}}$ at its last passing value if CV drops by more than 0.08 (\texttt{config.py}:327--395).

\subsection*{S2. Action mapping}

The policy outputs a raw, unbounded 9-vector $a$; $\sigma$ is the logistic sigmoid (\texttt{factory/environment.py}:479--531).

& Control & Mapping & Main-paper Table 1 \\
\midrule

\end{tabular}
\caption{Raw-action-to-control mappings. The $a_1$ range was narrowed from $0.5 + 2.5\,\sigma(a_1)$ on 2026-08-07; the extra-pass controls are deterministic given $a$, thresholded identically in training and evaluation, and stochastic only through Gaussian exploration during training.}
\end{table*}

Each policy step runs $n = 2 + e_1 + e_2$ chained generator passes. Pass $k = 0, \dots, n-1$ uses strength $\min(0.9,\ s\beta(1 + 0.15k))$, classifier scale $g_c\, b\,(1 + 0.35k)$ and preservation scale $g_p \max(1/b, 0.6)\max(1 - 0.15k, 0.4)$; the 0.6 floor never binds for $b \in (0.7, 1.3)$.

\subsection*{S5. What the repository does not record}

The committed runs post-date the final code snapshot (2026-08-07). In that snapshot \texttt{USE\_GUIDANCE\_ENSEMBLE} is \texttt{False}, whereas the committed runs used ensemble guidance, so the training configuration differs in at least that flag. The same commit reverted \texttt{BUDGET\_T} (8$\to$25), \texttt{FIXED\_EPISODE\_LENGTH} (\texttt{True}$\to$\texttt{False}), \texttt{EDIT\_BUDGET\_L1} (0.02$\to\infty$), \texttt{POLICY\_TRUNK} (conv$\to$MLP) and the LoRA adapter (rank 16 with a widened target set $\to$ rank 8); which values the committed runs used is not recorded. Also not in the repository: TODO: not found --- random seeds; the patient-level split procedure and ratio; the random-action and no-policy arm settings; the LHR implementation; the arm-level transfer and bootstrap code; the run manifests.
