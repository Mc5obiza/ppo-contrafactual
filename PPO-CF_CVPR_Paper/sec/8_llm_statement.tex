\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
\label{sec:llm-statement}

We used an LLM-based coding and writing assistant (Claude, Anthropic) extensively, under the authors' direction. (i) Code: it wrote much of the experiment and analysis code, including evaluation wrappers, the baselines' evaluation code, bootstrap and paired-test scripts, figure scripts and job-submission scripts. (ii) Experiments: in response to review feedback, it proposed, launched and analysed most of the follow-up experiments (the Latent Shift baseline, the LHR nulls, the transfer floor and ceiling, the seed replicates and the mammography LHR re-analysis). It also checked reported values against the logs and result files they came from. (iii) Writing: it drafted and revised large parts of the manuscript, tables and captions, and proposed framing changes, including the choice of in-lung LHR as the primary metric. The authors conceived the study and built the PPO-CF method and its original evaluation. They approved each experiment, framing decision and claim, and take full responsibility for the paper. No LLM was used to generate data, images or annotations, and no LLM is part of the method.
