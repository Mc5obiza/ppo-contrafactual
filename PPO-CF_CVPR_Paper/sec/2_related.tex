\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Counterfactuals for chest radiographs.} Singla \etal~\cite{singla2022explaining} introduced a GAN-based counterfactual generator for chest radiographs with the evaluation protocol (CV, FID and Foreign-Object Preservation, FOP) that became the de facto benchmark, which we adopt for comparability; FOP needs a detector trained on private annotations, so \S\ref{sec:conventional-metrics} uses a model-free substitute. Latent Shift~\cite{cohen2021gifsplanation} moves an autoencoder latent along the classifier gradient, RadEdit~\cite{perezgarcia2024radedit} edits radiographs with a diffusion model under multiple masks to stress-test classifiers, and FastDiME~\cite{weng2024fastdime} accelerates diffusion counterfactuals with self-optimized masks, including on chest X-ray shortcuts. Diffusion counterfactuals for natural images~\cite{augustin2022diffusion,jeanneret2022diffusion,jeanneret2023adversarial} and in latent space (LDCE~\cite{farid2023ldce}) likewise score validity with the target classifier.

\paragraph{Evaluating beyond the guidance classifier.} Re-scoring with a model not used for generation has precedents: DiME and ACE~\cite{jeanneret2022diffusion,jeanneret2023adversarial} count non-target attribute changes with an independently trained oracle, and DVCE~\cite{augustin2022diffusion} uses adversarially robust classifiers so that gradient-guided edits become semantic. Our transfer ratio re-scores the \emph{target} shift itself, as a per-direction ratio to the guidance shift on validators trained on other corpora, read against a matched attack floor and a real-image ceiling rather than a cut-off; the novelty is the calibrated pairing with CV and with a lesion anchor, not re-scoring as such. LHR adapts the energy-based pointing game of Score-CAM~\cite{wang2020scorecam} (the share of a map's energy inside a box) from saliency maps to the counterfactual difference map, with an in-lung area null that a lesion-blind edit cannot beat (\S\ref{sec:lhr}).

\paragraph{Diffusion editing and spatial locks.} Classifier-guided diffusion~\cite{dhariwal2021diffusion,meng2022sdedit,preechakul2022diffusion} has displaced GANs for editable generation. We build on a LoRA-adapted~\cite{hu2022lora} Stable Diffusion v1.5~\cite{rombach2022highresolution} backbone with DDIM sampling~\cite{song2021denoising}. RePaint~\cite{lugmayr2022repaint} and Blended Latent Diffusion~\cite{avrahami2022blended} re-composite the unedited region at every step; we adapt this as a soft lock through a dilated, feathered lung mask.

\paragraph{Localized and adaptive counterfactuals.} ECED~\cite{luu2025visual} edits a Score-CAM foreground and DocVCE~\cite{saifullah2025docvce} filters adversarial gradients and restores unneeded patches. FiRe~\cite{zeng2026fire} is closest in mechanism: it refines at a fixed noise level under saliency dual masks and \emph{hand-designs} the controls PPO-CF learns, guidance that strengthens when more editing is needed and weakens near the target decision, and early stopping once the decision flips. PPO-CF instead learns per-step strength, guidance scales, pass count and stopping from a reward, around a lung rather than a saliency mask (a Grad-CAM mask collapsed our task, \S\ref{sec:negative-results}). Our finding that a fixed schedule at the policy's mean action recovers most of its gain (\S\ref{sec:main-results}) suggests a hand-designed controller of FiRe's kind may suffice. None of these three methods reports a lesion-anchored metric or held-out target transfer, and none was run on our split and seeding, so we do not compare head-to-head (Supp.~E3).

\paragraph{RL and schedules for diffusion.} Most RL work on diffusion fine-tunes the weights against a reward (\eg DDPO~\cite{black2024training}). Sampling schedules have been optimized without RL, for noise levels (Align Your Steps~\cite{sabour2024align}) and for guidance weights over time~\cite{wang2024cfgschedulers}, where simple monotone schedules are hard to beat. Learning per-step sampling and guidance controls with RL around a frozen generator, for counterfactual editing, is less explored; in our setting the learned controls turn out close to a fixed schedule.
