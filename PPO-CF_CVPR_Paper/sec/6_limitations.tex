\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{One dataset, two generators.} Every result is on RSNA Pneumonia. Besides our system we ran Latent Shift and a CycleGAN under the identical protocol; DiME, LDCE and robust-classifier methods need RSNA-trained models that do not exist (Supp.~E3). The CV gap is established for two systems, not for the field. An exploratory mammography port supports LHR in both directions, but its guidance classifier saw most evaluation images and no validator reaches AUROC 0.65 (Supp.~E9). No radiologist assessed the counterfactuals, and we make no clinical claim.
    \item \textbf{Edits remain diffuse.} No arm we trained moved edit concentration meaningfully; every one sits in the same 0.21--0.31 band. The lock keeps at least 93\% of the change relative to the VAE round trip inside the editable lung region (Supp.~E6), so the diffuseness is within the lung. Whether diffuseness is fixable within a gradient-guidance approach is open.
    \item \textbf{Transfer anchors are approximate.} The ceiling compares populations, not paired edits, and the norm-matched attack is large enough to be partly semantic (\S\ref{sec:cv-overstates}). We therefore claim only that n$\to$p transfer does not exceed the attack floor, not that the edits are adversarial.
    \item \textbf{Classifier overlap with the evaluation data.} The \texttt{rsna} guidance model and the FID extractor were trained on RSNA data, and \texttt{nih} on NIH ChestX-ray14, which contains RSNA's images (\S\ref{sec:transfer}); these models may have seen our test images, which can inflate CV and the \texttt{nih} ratio. \texttt{chex}, \texttt{pc} and \texttt{mimic\_ch} have no RSNA exposure. Generator fine-tuning, PPO rollouts and the validity probe use only the 80\% training fold.
    \item \textbf{Few training runs per arm.} The default and single-classifier configurations have three training runs each, but only the PPO seed varies (Supp.~E10). The other arms have one run each, so their gaps have no run-to-run error bar. Adapter, group, validator and seeding departures that affect individual comparisons are listed in \S\ref{sec:caveats}.
    \item \textbf{Adaptivity is limited.} In n$\to$p, 199 of 200 episodes end after one step, with strength, pass count and guidance bias at or near fixed values; in p$\to$n the median episode is one step and 67 to 78\% of the action variance in multi-step episodes is between images (Supp.~R3). A fixed schedule matches the policy (\S\ref{sec:controller}), so per-image adaptation adds nothing we can detect. The 37.9M-parameter policy was trained on at most $800 \times 25$ transitions per direction.
    \item \textbf{Sample size and proxies.} The 3-model arm's p$\to$n group is small ($n=32$, 27 annotated; 12 shared for LHR). FID at $n \leq 200$ in a 1024-d space is high-variance (Supp.~E6). FOP is not computable offline, so IP measures a related property, and SCP is degenerate in this configuration.
    \item \textbf{Not measured:} convergence curves (two logged points per direction); a LoRA-rank ablation; Score-CAM or consensus masks; a wider $a_7$ range; n$\to$p lesion targeting (LHR needs annotated sources; a second dataset with boxes, such as VinDr-CXR, would also anchor it). The lock width was not tuned (exploratory check in Supp.~E4).
\end{itemize}
